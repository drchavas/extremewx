Idealized Tropical Cyclone Track & Wind Swath

Build a storm track from scratch: type a latitude, longitude, Vmax and R34kt for each point — or drag the points around the map — and the full wind swath is generated from the tcwindprofile model. Click any track point for its wind profile and derived Rmax, Pmin and wind radii. Same swath rendering as the real-storm viewer; see also the single-storm wind field explorer. Work in progress — if you see a bug please email me.
