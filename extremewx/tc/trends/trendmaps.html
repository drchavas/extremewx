Read trends with care. Best-track intensity is not a measurement. Outside the Atlantic there is rarely aircraft reconnaissance, so Vmax comes from Dvorak satellite analysis, whose sensors and procedures have changed since 1980. Counts above a fixed threshold are less exposed than intensity values. Winds here are the USA (NHC / JTWC) 1-minute values; the WMO fields are not used because their availability climbs from 55% to 85% across this record, which alone would look like a trend.
