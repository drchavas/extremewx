Extreme Weather Climatology and Trends — 2° grid

The same paired climatology and trend maps as the county version, binned into 2° × 2° boxes. Drag or zoom either map and both follow. Click a box to read its series below. State and county lines are geographic reference only; the Place menu just re-centres the maps. Coarser cells trade detail for far more reports each, which makes trends steadier.
