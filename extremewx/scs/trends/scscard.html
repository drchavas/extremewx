Severe Convective Storm Trend Cards

Where hail, tornadoes and thunderstorm winds happen, where that is changing, how the change is distributed with latitude and longitude, and how the regional total has evolved — in one frame, on a 2° grid over the lower 48. A hazard day is a calendar day on which a grid cell saw at least one report meeting the chosen threshold. Prefer county resolution? · Prefer a pannable map? · Email me with bugs or requests.
