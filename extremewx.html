Tropical Cyclones

Historical Event Viewer
  • TC Viewer — Global Track & Wind-Field Explorer Now a dedicated site at tcviewer.org. Any storm worldwide in the IBTrACS record (all basins, NHC/JTWC) — best-track coloured by Saffir–Simpson category with 34/64 kt wind-field swaths and Rmax; click any point for full data and its size vs. basin climatology. Or pick a date to see every storm active that day, with its position at 00/06/12/18 UTC.
Historical Trends
  • Climatology & Trends — 5° grid Paired climatology and trend maps on a 5° global grid, each with a zonal profile beside it drawn on the same latitude axis, plus the region’s annual series, its seasonal cycle and all twelve months below. Track density, storm count, ACE, intensity, central pressure and storm size (radius of 34-kt winds), 1980–2025 from IBTrACS best tracks. Click any basin on the map to zoom into it — nine regions that exactly tile the globe, including the Australian region — choose which lifecycle stages count, from tropical only to every stage, and switch a 5° Gaussian smoother on or off. Opens on 1990, after which the global record is reasonably homogeneous. Exports as one figure.
Research (experimental)
  • TC Wind Field Explorer Build an idealized storm and watch its wind footprint, radial profile and pressure respond live.
  • Idealized TC Track & Wind Swath Draw a storm track by hand and see the full wind swath it produces.
  • TC Ventilation Explorer How wind shear and dry mid-level air set the ventilated potential intensity, genesis threshold and intensification rate.
  • TC Potential Size The outer size a storm is trying to reach, set by its environment alone.
  • TC Potential Intensity Explorer ↗ new tab, external site A hurricane's wind-speed limit as a heat engine, computed from an editable sounding and SST. Co-developed with Jonathan Lin (Cornell).
  • Atlantic TC Landfall Risk Explorer Strike probability at any North Atlantic point by intensity, radius and month. Still very experimental.

Severe Thunderstorms

Historical Event Viewer
  • Historical Severe Weather Events Individual storms rather than climatology. Step through the SPC derecho archive — 184 wind swaths, 1956–2025, each with its published track and the reports inside it — or the biggest hail, tornado and thunderstorm-wind days on record: top 50 for any state, top 100 nationally, ranked by how many counties reported the hazard. Map any day as shaded counties or individual reports, or type any date directly.
Historical Trends
  • Climatology & Trends — county level Paired climatology and trend maps at U.S. county resolution on one shared view — drag or zoom either and both follow. Click a state to centre on it or a county to pin it, with an annual series and all twelve months below. Hail, tornado, thunderstorm wind, derecho, freezing rain and peak wind, with an optional 2° Gaussian smoother.
  • Climatology & Trends — 2° grid The same paired maps binned into a regular 2° grid, so every box carries comparable area rather than the wildly uneven sizes of U.S. counties. Click any box for its own series; states and counties are drawn as geographic reference only. Hail, tornado, thunderstorm wind and derecho.
Research (experimental)
  • SCS Environmental Sounding Explorer Build a severe-storm environment from thermodynamic and kinematic parameters; skew-T, hodograph and every index update live. Chavas & Dawson (2021).
  • Sounding Plotter Plot and edit any sounding — paste, type or drag the curves; CAPE, CIN, shear, SRH and composite indices update live.

Climate

  • Weather & Climate Data Visualization Animated monthly ERA5 climatologies — temperature, SST, pressure, clouds, precipitation, moisture and winds aloft — showing how Earth's atmosphere breathes through the seasons.
