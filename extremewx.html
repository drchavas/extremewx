Extreme Weather Visualizations

Interactive maps and explorers for extreme weather. Many things a work in progress, new updates expected regularly. Email me if there is a feature you would like to see added!

In development · experimental · not yet official

Tropical Cyclones

Historical Event Viewer
  • IBTrACS TC Track & Wind Footprint Map Explorer Any Atlantic storm since 1851 — best-track by Saffir–Simpson category with 34/64 kt wind swaths; click any point for full data.
Historical Trends
  • Tropical Cyclone Trends Global climatology and long-term trends of TC activity from IBTrACS, in one exportable frame.
Research
  • TC Wind Field Explorer Build an idealized storm and watch its wind footprint, radial profile and pressure respond live.
  • Idealized TC Track & Wind Swath Draw a storm track by hand and see the full wind swath it produces.
  • TC Ventilation Explorer How wind shear and dry mid-level air set the ventilated potential intensity, genesis threshold and intensification rate.
  • TC Potential Size (experimental) The outer size a storm is trying to reach, set by its environment alone. Experimental — known errors, work in progress.
  • TC Potential Intensity Explorer ↗ new tab, external site A hurricane's wind-speed limit as a heat engine, computed from an editable sounding and SST. Co-developed with Jonathan Lin (MIT).
  • Atlantic TC Landfall Risk Explorer (experimental) Strike probability at any North Atlantic point by intensity, radius and month.

Severe Thunderstorms

Historical Event Viewer
  • Historical Severe Weather Events Individual storms rather than climatology — the SPC derecho archive (1956–2025) and the biggest hail, tornado and thunderstorm-wind days on record.
Historical Trends
  • Climatology & Trends — county level Paired climatology and trend maps at U.S. county resolution, with annual and month-by-month series. Hail, tornado, thunderstorm wind, freezing rain and peak wind.
  • Climatology & Trends — 2° grid The same paired maps binned into a regular 2° grid.
Research
  • SCS Environmental Sounding Explorer Build a severe-storm environment from thermodynamic and kinematic parameters; skew-T, hodograph and every index update live. Chavas & Dawson (2021).
  • Sounding Plotter Plot and edit any sounding — paste, type or drag the curves; CAPE, CIN, shear, SRH and composite indices update live.

Also: Weather & Climate Data Visualization — animated seasonal cycles of Earth's atmosphere and surface climate (ERA5).
