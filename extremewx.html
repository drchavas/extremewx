Tropical Cyclones

7 explorers

Best-track data, wind fields, wind-field structure, energetics, size, long-term trends, and landfall risk.

Historical: IBTrACS TC Track & Wind Footprint Map Explorer

Pick any Atlantic storm since 1851 and view its best-track colored by Saffir–Simpson category, with 34 kt and 64 kt wind-field swaths. Click any point for full data at that timestep.

Tropical cyclone trends

The whole story of one field in a single frame: climatology and trend maps with their zonal profiles, the annual series and a month-by-month grid. Exports cleanly to PNG or SVG for a talk or paper.

Open explorer

Research: TC Wind Field Explorer

Build an idealized storm from Vmax and outer size and watch the wind footprint, radial profile and pressure respond live. Shows how Vmax, Rmax, R34kt and Pmin are related, using a published physics-based model.

Open explorer

Research: Idealized TC Track & Wind Swath

Build a storm track by hand — type lat, lon, Vmax and R34kt for each point, or drag them around the map — and see the full wind swath it produces, with optional motion asymmetry. Click any point for its wind profile.

Open explorer

Research: TC Ventilation Explorer

Wind shear feeds dry mid-level air into a storm's core as "anti-fuel." Set the potential intensity, shear and mid-level dryness and watch the whole phase space of intensity change respond — the ventilated PI ceiling, the genesis threshold, and the predicted rate of intensification.

Open explorer

Research: TC Potential Size

Why are storms the size they are? Latent heating drives an inflow that grows a storm outward, but on a sphere the β gyres ventilate the vortex and spin its outer circulation back down. Set latitude, SST and the storm parameters and watch the f-plane prediction run away as 1/f while the β-plane equilibrium holds near 500–700 km, as observed. Experimental — known errors, work in progress.

Open explorer

Research: TC Potential Intensity Explorer (co-developed with Jonathan Lin)

Compute a hurricane's potential intensity — its wind-speed limit as a heat engine running on ocean warmth — directly from an editable sounding and sea-surface temperature, and see the CAPE construction behind Emanuel's PI theory.

Open explorer (separate window, external site)

Atlantic TC Landfall Risk Explorer (experimental)

Drop a marker anywhere in the North Atlantic and see strike probability by intensity, radius, and month. Explore how landfall risk evolves through the hurricane season.

Severe Convective Storms

3 explorers

The environments that produce supercells and tornadoes — built from their physical ingredients or read straight off a sounding — and the observed U.S. record of hail, wind, and tornado activity.

Severe convective storm trends

Climatology and trend side by side on one shared map — drag or zoom either and both follow, so the pair always shows the same ground. Click a state to centre on it, or a county to pin it, with annual and month-by-month series below. Covers hail, tornado, thunderstorm wind, freezing rain and peak wind.

Open explorer

Sounding Plotter

Plot and edit any sounding. Paste a University of Wyoming table, a spreadsheet or a CM1 input_sounding, edit levels directly, or drag the temperature and dewpoint curves on the plot — CAPE, CIN, shear, SRH and the composite indices update live.

Open explorer

Research: SCS Environmental Sounding Explorer

Build a severe-storm environment from eight thermodynamic and six kinematic parameters and watch the skew-T, the static-energy profile, the hodograph and every index respond live. Uses the physics-based model of Chavas and Dawson (2021); exports a CM1 input_sounding.

Open explorer
